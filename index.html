🧱 BlockVerse

A mini-Roblox 3D obby & sandbox. Run, jump, bounce, collect studs, hit checkpoints and grab the trophy!

🕹 Mobile: left stick to move • drag right side to look • ⤒ to jump
⌨️ Desktop: WASD / arrows • Space jump • drag to look • B build • X delete • R respawn
🧱 Build: pick a color below, tap 🧱+ to place a block ahead of you

How to play

  • Collect gold studs for score.
  • Touch flags to save checkpoints.
  • Avoid lava — you respawn at the last flag.
  • Green pads bounce you high. Red bar spins — time it!
  • Reach the 🏆 trophy to win.
